We suspected batching was hurting our labels. It was the buy date.

DeepSeek v4-flash labels Steam reviews 10 per prompt to amortize a 9K-token codebook — a 10× cost lever that came under suspicion when the batched census labels scored 0.033 F1 below a lab run of the same configuration. Five controlled re-label runs (5,693 fresh labels, decision rules fixed before any data) split the two suspects: vary one thing, hold the other, and watch the change in agreement (ΔF1).
