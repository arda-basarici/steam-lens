1
Data
A 49-game corpus filtered to every English non-empty review. Ingest asserts the ruled census size and fails loud before any money moves.
2
Labeling engine
Per-review aspect + sentiment classification through a provider-agnostic seam. The labeler was picked by a frozen-metric bake-off, not reputation; the whole census cost $3.80.
3
Number mint
A pure fold from labels to per-game aggregates. Only survey-origin, version-pinned labels may enter — the two-track wall is enforced by structure, not convention.
4
Evaluation instrument
Human-anchored gold, a cross-family judge calibrated before use, and agreement measured beyond gold's reach. Every published number carries its confidence interval.
5
Evals gate CI
The runs of record regenerate in CI from committed artifacts; an exact-digit mismatch fails the build. Tolerance bands exist too — as the label re-buy rule, floored at measured buy-time variance.
