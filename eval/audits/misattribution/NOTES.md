# Misattribution audit — pass notes

Audited 2026-08-05 by Arda (100 primary claims; 2 non-English primaries
SKIPped and replaced from the ordered reserves — items 042, 100 → reserves
1, 2). Verdict-frame rulings made during the pass, recorded for the report:

1. **The quote is judged in its review's context** (the sheet's stated rule,
   affirmed after discussion): the quote decides *where* the claim points,
   the surrounding review decides *what it means there*. A thin span whose
   context settles the sentiment is `yes`; a thin span context cannot settle
   is `unclear`. Span brevity alone is never a `no`.
2. **The two fields are independent**: an aspect failure is never
   double-counted into sentiment (claim 030 — wrong pin, but the positive is
   gleefully carried).
3. **Wish-rule**: a suggestion/wish for an *absent* feature does not talk
   about the claimed aspect → `aspect: no` (034 mods, 049 multiplayer). A
   more-of-existing request too terse to read either way → `unclear` (069).
4. **Routing ≠ misattribution at the candidate slot**: a candidate label
   whose words match the quote's subject is supported even where a pinned
   neighbor exists (076 "space exploration") — the pin-vs-candidate gap is
   the ontology alias/promotion loop's business, not a wrong attachment.
5. **Ambiguous referent syntax → `unclear`** (027 — two grammatical parses
   with different labels; the disagreement between two readers was itself
   the evidence).

The headline finding beside the rate: the misattributions are dominated by
close-family routing (bugs↔stability, updates↔developer_conduct,
ai_behavior↔balance number-tuning), not wild misreads; sentiment
misattribution is rare (3/98 definite). Run of record in `report.json`
beside this file (sheet + sample pinned by sha256).
